Guides for building your gaming PC

Everything you need to know to build your own gaming PC with confidence, explained from scratch without unnecessary jargon.

Where to start when building a gaming PC? These guides cover, in order, everything you need to decide before buying: what each component is (glossary), how to make sure the parts are compatible with each other (socket, RAM type, case size, PSU wattage), and how to choose each individual part — GPU, CPU, RAM, and power supply — based on your budget and what you'll use the PC for.

These aren't generic guides: they're written for people building their first PC who want to avoid the most costly mistakes (a cheap PSU, a CPU/GPU bottleneck, running short on RAM). If you'd rather jump straight to a ready-made budget, use the configurator and pick your tier: entry, mid-range, high-end, or extreme.
