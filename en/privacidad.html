Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At ArmaPC we respect your privacy. This policy clearly explains what information is collected when you visit the site, how it's used, and what choices you have.

1. Who we are

ArmaPC is an informational website dedicated to helping people build their own gaming PC. You can reach us at contacto@tupcgamer.com for any questions related to this policy.

2. What information we collect

This site doesn't ask you to create an account or register personal data to use it. The information collected is mostly technical and automatic, such as what cookies and analytics tools gather: browser type, pages visited, and general, anonymous usage data that helps us understand what content is useful.

Some preferences you choose (like the currency you view prices in) are saved locally in your own browser to improve your experience. That information never leaves your device or reaches us.

3. Cookies

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. This site uses cookies to remember your preferences and to show relevant ads through third parties (see the next section). You can disable or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time; keep in mind that some features may stop remembering your preferences if you do.

4. Third-party advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.

  • Google uses the DART cookie (or equivalents) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • Third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to gather information about your visits in order to show you ads about products and services that may interest you.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or disable third-party cookies through www.aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, you can check Google's privacy policy for partner sites.

5. Links to other sites

This site may include links to stores or other external websites, for example so you can buy the components we recommend. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; we recommend reading their own policies when you visit them.

6. Your rights

ArmaPC is built for people across the Americas, so visitors may be subject to different data protection laws depending on their country of residence. In general, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data collected about you, as well as to object to certain uses, in accordance with whatever data protection regulations apply to you. For example, if you visit us from Peru, these rights are recognized under Law No. 29733, the Personal Data Protection Law, and its regulations; other countries in the region have their own equivalent regulations. If you have questions or want to exercise these rights, write to us at contacto@tupcgamer.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of the page. We recommend checking back occasionally.
